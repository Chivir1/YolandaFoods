# YOLANDA FOODS - Step 1: Visual Identity & Color Palette Specification

## 1. Brand Concept & Aesthetic Mood
- **Brand Name**: YOLANDA FOODS
- **Vibe**: Fresh, Organic, High-Energy, Natural Farm-to-Table, Youthful & Modern.
- **Logo Character**: 
  - Wordmark: Plump, bubbly, friendly, rounded letterforms ("YOLANDA FOODS") with smooth liquid highlights and soft organic curves.
  - Accent Leaf: Crisp, geometric 3-point natural leaf perched at the top corner of the wordmark (sharp, non-bubbly, delicate).
  - Splash Elements: Dynamic dynamic fluid splashes at 3 corners with drops and droplets in 3 distinct colors (Primary Green, Fresh Light Green, and Punchy Splash Red).
  - Form: Free-floating (no enclosing box, circle, or rigid geometric badge).

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

1. **Logo & Expressive Display**:
   - **Type Style**: Bubbly, rounded, ultra-bold soft-edge display font (e.g., custom SVG vector lettering + *Fredoka / Poppins Bold* hybrid for sub-text).
   - **Weight**: 800 - 900 (Extra Bold / Heavy rounded).
2. **Headings & Section Titles**:
   - **Font**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Outfit* (Geometric, friendly, modern, premium).
   - **Weights**: 700 (Bold) and 600 (SemiBold).
3. **Body & Product Information**:
   - **Font**: *Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans*.
   - **Weight**: 400 (Regular) and 500 (Medium) for high readability on small mobile screens.
4. **Price Display & Badges**:
   - **Font**: Tabular Numbers Bold, clear ₦ Naira currency symbol formatting.

## 4. Alight Motion Kinetic Motion Parameters (Preview for Step 3)
- **Entrance Style**: Elastic pop-in with fluid liquid burst (squash-and-stretch easing: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`).
- **Splash Timing**: Corner splashes burst outward in staggered offsets (0ms, 120ms, 240ms) with small trailing droplet physics.
- **Leaf Placement**: Top-right corner of the wordmark, entering with a crisp gentle rotation snap (0 to 12 degrees) contrasting with the bubbly text.
- **Backdrop transition**: Smooth soft vignette blur dissolving into the clean storefront.
